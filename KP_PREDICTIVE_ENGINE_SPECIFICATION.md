# PART I: KNOWLEDGE EXTRACTION & CORE PRINCIPLES

## 1. Core KP Chain
**House → Occupant → Star Lord → Sub Lord → Timing**

- **House**: Area/event under consideration.
- **Occupant & Stellar Connections**: Provide raw house significations.
- **Star Lord**: Indicates the principal field/matter through which a planet operates.
- **Sub Lord**: Decides whether the indicated matter is favourable or unfavourable.
- **Cuspal Sub Lord**: Critical for establishing the foundational promise of a particular house/event.
- **Dasa / Bhukti / Anthra / Sukshma / Prana**: Provide the 5-level hierarchical timing layers.
- **Transit**: Provides activation and fructification triggers.

The source stresses that a Star can indicate multiple matters, while the Sub division helps eliminate alternatives and select the definitive result.

## 2. Cuspal Sub Lord
For each relevant cusp retain:
- Cusp number and exact longitude
- Star Lord
- Sub Lord
- Houses signified by the Sub Lord
- Supportive vs. obstructive role

The source provides concrete examples where a planet becomes a strong significator because it is the Sub Lord of a relevant cusp, even when ordinary bhava analysis alone would not make it dominant.

## 3. Significator Evidence
Preserve provenance rather than flattening significators into arbitrary scores:
1. **Grade 1**: Planet in the Star of an occupant of the relevant house.
2. **Grade 2**: Planet occupying the relevant house.
3. **Grade 3**: Planet in the Star of the lord of the house.
4. **Grade 4**: Lord of the house.
5. **Cusp Sub Lord**: Sub Lord of the relevant cusp.
6. **Rahu / Ketu Representation**: The nodes act as strongest agents for the planets they conjoin, aspect, or whose sign they occupy.

Recommended evidence fields: `planet`, `houses`, `reason/source`, `strength` (`Grade_1` to `Grade_4`), `supporting text/page`.

## 4. Promise Before Timing
Strict separation of **event promise** from **event timing**.

**Pipeline:**
$$\text{Event} \longrightarrow \text{Relevant Houses/Cusps} \longrightarrow \text{Cusp Sub Lords} \longrightarrow \text{Significators} \longrightarrow \text{Promise Test} \longrightarrow \text{Periods} \longrightarrow \text{Transit} \longrightarrow \text{Event Window}$$

> **Cardinal Rule:** A favourable transit or running Dasa must never manufacture an event whose natal cuspal promise is absent or obstructed.

## 5. Dasa / Bhukti / Anthra / Sukshma / Prana
Evaluate each period lord against the required event houses and significators. The nested period layers progressively narrow the timing; they must never be treated as generic planet meanings detached from event signification.

## 6. Transit
$$\text{Transiting Planet} \longrightarrow \text{Transit Star Lord (Matter/Field)} \longrightarrow \text{Transit Sub Lord (Favourable/Unfavourable Result)}$$

Retain transiting planet, Star, Star Lord, Sub, signified houses, current period lords, and event compatibility.

## 8. Finance and Debt
The source distinguishes:
- **2nd**: Money / bank position / liquid cash / movable wealth
- **6th**: Loans / borrowed money / service-related dues
- **10th**: Professional income
- **11th**: Net gain / profit
- **12th**: Expenditure / capital outflow

A planet can produce apparently contradictory results such as receipt and payment when connected with both 11 and 12. Both indications must be preserved.

For debt, distinguish loan received, outstanding debt, repayment, closure, and loss after borrowing. The source uses Saturn as an example of delay/prolongation without necessarily implying denial.

## 9. Property
The source connects 4th with building/property, Mars with building/land, 2nd with liquid money, 1st with effort, and 12th with spending/investment. It describes acquisition through purchase, inheritance/legacy, and borrowing/investment depending on connections.

## 10. Education and Overseas
The source uses 4th for education, Mercury for intellect, 9th for higher studies/long journey, 12th for foreign residence, and 11th as gain in the scholarship example. Scholarship logic must remain distinct from ordinary education.

## 11. Business and Litigation
The source gives a definitive Sub rule:
- **Same Star + Favourable Sub** $\longrightarrow$ Success / gain / accomplishment.
- **Same Star + Adverse Sub** $\longrightarrow$ Loss / difficulty / failure.

For litigation, an indication of litigation through the Star can still lead to victory or loss depending on the Sub.
For business, a favourable Sub promises profit while an adverse Sub threatens loss.

## 12. Rahu / Ketu Representation
Nodes must preserve their full representation chain rather than being treated merely as standalone planets:
$$\text{Node} \longrightarrow \text{Sign Lord} \longrightarrow \text{Star Lord} \longrightarrow \text{Sub Lord} \longrightarrow \text{Represented / Connected Planets} \longrightarrow \text{Houses}$$

## 13. Contradiction Resolver
Do not discard contradictory significations. Represent:
- Supporting houses
- Obstructing houses
- Mixed factors
- Dominant evidence
- Interpretation: `SUPPORTED` / `MIXED` / `OBSTRUCTED` / `INCONCLUSIVE`

Distinguish **Delay vs. Denial**. The source explicitly illustrates Saturn as capable of prolonging an outcome rather than automatically denying it.

## 14. Ruling Planets / Prashna
The source uses ruling planets at the moment of query when exact birth information is unavailable:
$$\text{Question Moment} \longrightarrow \text{Ruling Planets} \longrightarrow \text{Relevant Cusp / Sub} \longrightarrow \text{Significators} \longrightarrow \text{Promise} \longrightarrow \text{Timing}$$

Keep query-based analysis separate from natal prediction.

## 15. Rectification
Because cusps can change over short time intervals, rectification must use multiple known events, relevant cusp degree/Star/Sub, event significators, and ruling planets. Treat rectification as an evidence-backed hypothesis, never certainty from one single event.

## 16. Explainability
Every prediction must transparently answer:
1. Which houses represent the event?
2. Which cusp was examined?
3. Who is the cusp Sub Lord?
4. Which houses does that Sub Lord signify?
5. Which significators support it?
6. Which period activates it?
7. Which transit triggers it?
8. Why favourable, unfavourable, or mixed?
9. Is it delayed rather than denied?
10. What contradicts it?

## 18. Remedies Layer
The predictive source material does not establish modern scientifically validated remedies. Traditional remedies must remain in a separate, source-attributed traditional layer:
$$\text{Prediction} \longrightarrow \text{Astrological Cause / Symbolism} \longrightarrow \text{Traditional Remedy} \longrightarrow \text{Classical Source} \longrightarrow \text{Non-Guarantee Disclaimer}$$

Never present mantra, gemstone, donation, or ritual as guaranteed cures or guaranteed worldly outcomes.

## 19. Medical Safety Boundary
The source explicitly cautions against discouraging medical predictions and specifically advises against predicting death. The product must strictly avoid death-date predictions and medical diagnoses, framing health astrology as interpretive rather than medical fact.

# PART II: ALL-IN-ONE IMPLEMENTATION SPECIFICATION

## 0. Master KP Model

Prediction operates as an unbroken evidence chain:
$$\text{Cusp} \longrightarrow \text{Occupant} \longrightarrow \text{Star Lord} \longrightarrow \text{Sub Lord} \longrightarrow \text{Significators} \longrightarrow \text{Event Promise} \longrightarrow \text{Dasa Hierarchy (5 Levels)} \longrightarrow \text{Transit Star/Sub} \longrightarrow \text{Result}$$

Each constellation is divided into nine unequal Sub divisions in Vimshottari proportions. The Sub sequence begins with the constellation lord and follows the planetary cycle.

## 1.3 House Significators
Build a **significator graph**, not a flat list:
```ts

  reason: string;
  sourceBook?: string;
  sourcePage?: number;

```
Do not collapse all significators into one undifferentiated score. AstroLife must preserve **why** a planet signifies a house.

  starLord?: string;

Node interpretation must remain traceable. If multiple representation paths exist, preserve all of them rather than silently selecting one.

8. **Transit alone does not create a natal promise.**
